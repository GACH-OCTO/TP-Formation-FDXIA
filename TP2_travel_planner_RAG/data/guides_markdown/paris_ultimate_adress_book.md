## Guide des arrondissements de Paris

Central Paris is divided into 20 arrondissements, or neighbourhoods, each with their own character and personality. Because all arrondissements will have decent local eateries and interesting shops, you will likely be eating and shopping locally at least some of the time. I’ve therefore focused my attention on the arrondissements that are where you are likely to be staying, or at least visiting as you travel around Paris.
